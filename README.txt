HIFI TECHNOLOGY WEBSITE - QUICK START

1. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox to preview the landing page.
2. Upload the complete hifi-technology-website folder to your hosting account (keep the assets folder).
3. Edit index.html and replace the demo shop details:
   - +91 98765 43210 (phone number)
   - info@hifitechnology.in (email)
   - 123, Main Road, Avoor, Tiruvannamalai - 606XXX (address)
   - Social links currently point to the contact section; replace with your real profile URLs.
4. Edit script.js and change WHATSAPP_NUMBER to your WhatsApp number, country code first, digits only.
5. Replace the brand text in index.html with your final shop name and add your logo if available.
6. Replace product image URLs in index.html with your actual product / installation photos for best customer trust.

IMPORTANT
- Contact details and product details in this project are DEMO placeholders. Verify and replace them before publishing.
- The enquiry form opens WhatsApp with the entered details. It does not save enquiries to a database or email them automatically.
- Google Fonts, Font Awesome and the product photos are loaded from external CDNs / image URLs, so an internet connection is required for those assets.
- The generated design-reference.png is a visual concept preview, not the live webpage itself.
